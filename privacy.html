Privacy policy

This is a personal home-automation project used only by its owner.

It accesses the owner's Google account data (such as Google Drive and Google Calendar) only to run the owner's own backups and calendar sync on the owner's own devices.

No data is collected by anyone else, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Data stays in the owner's Google account and on the owner's own devices.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
